# InstaStore MVP Scope

This document defines the approved minimum viable product for InstaStore.

## Positioning

InstaStore is a storefront and order-management product for Instagram-first merchants. The MVP targets merchants in Abuja and Lagos.

## In Scope

### Tenancy and accounts

- Multi-store tenancy: multiple merchants each run their own storefront.
- One merchant account per store initially.
- Public store slug URLs to reach each storefront.

### Catalog and inventory

- Product management.
- Inventory management.
- Primary product image plus gallery images.
- Basic sale pricing.

### Customers and delivery

- Customer search and filtering.
- Abuja and Lagos delivery zones.
- Merchant-defined delivery fees.
- Merchant-defined logistics provider per zone.

### Commerce

- Bank-transfer checkout.
- Receipt upload.
- Merchant payment verification.
- Order tracking.

### Storefront and branding

- Store branding.
- Consent.
- Attribution.

### Analytics

- walkerOS.
- Umami.

## Out of Scope (Non-Goals)

- Payment gateway.
- Logistics API.
- Maps.
- Custom domains.
- Staff roles.
- Customer accounts.
- Jitsu.
- Microservices.
- Full promotion engine.
- Complex variants.
- CRM.
- Accounting.
- POS.
- AI features.

## Account Provisioning

First merchants use assisted account provisioning.

Self-service signup comes after the first merchant cohort.
